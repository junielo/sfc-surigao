CFC SFC Surigao del Norte is a community of Young Catholic Adults ages 21-40 from SDN.
We are part of the global community of CFC Singles for Christ which is under the Family Ministries of Couples for Christ.

Christian Life Program

A 13-session course, usually weekly, with a teaching, group discussion, and fellowship, about 2.5 hours each.

Households

A small group of 5 to 7 people who meet weekly in members' homes for prayer, sharing, and fellowship.

Prayer Assemblies & Teachings

A monthly gathering of the whole chapter for worship, Scripture, and sharing, plus teachings on topics like courtship, time management, and the Theology of the Body.

Events & Mission

Large gatherings like the 30th anniversary, with a praise parade, a talent show, a TikTok dance contest, sports tournaments, and an acoustic concert, plus service.

Start your journey with Christ

Talks, sharing, worship, and fellowship, all in a welcoming community.
